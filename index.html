Use the stricter of your agency's limits and the federal limits. This drill uses a sample county policy: micro-purchases up to $10,000, and formal solicitation with Board approval above $200,000.

Also check: Appendix II contract clauses (termination above $10,000), a SAM.gov exclusion check on every vendor (this agency requires it on every purchase; the federal floor is $25,000), outreach to small, minority-owned and women-owned firms, no cost-plus-a-percentage-of-cost pricing, and a ceiling on any time-and-materials contract. Federal thresholds reflect the FAR adjustment effective October 1, 2025.
